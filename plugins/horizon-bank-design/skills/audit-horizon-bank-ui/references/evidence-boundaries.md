# Evidence boundaries

Use these labels in review notes:

- **Horizon identity requirement**: directly supported by bundled Horizon logos, tokens, project terminology, or explicit product architecture.
- **Authorization requirement**: necessary to preserve the deterministic bank boundary, customer approval integrity, credential secrecy, or independent revocation controls.
- **Accessibility requirement**: a WCAG 2.2 criterion or clear semantic/platform requirement.
- **Implementation recommendation**: a practical system choice, not a policy violation.
- **Needs governance confirmation**: requires product, legal, compliance, privacy, security, trademark, or accessibility approval.

Do not report a recommendation as a policy violation. Do not treat one screen’s CSS as a universal rule. When evidence is missing, say what must be confirmed and still provide a safe default.
